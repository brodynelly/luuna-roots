Usage: luuna-roots scan [<id>]

Find mentions in edits made outside a session

Scans ideas whose idea.md changed since its last recorded hash (plant, think
session end, or a previous scan) and files link proposals for mentions of other
ideas (slug, id, or a distinctive phrase from their statement).

Options:
      --all  Scan every idea, changed or not

Global options:
      --json   Machine-readable JSON output
  -q, --quiet  Suppress non-essential output
  -h, --help   Show help for this command
