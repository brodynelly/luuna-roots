Usage: luuna-roots show <id>

Show an idea or sprout with edges, questions and history

<id> may be r-a1b2, a1b2, a slug, or a unique prefix of any of them.
For ideas, also lists linked Missions issues (.luuna/missions/issues.jsonl: an `intent` field, else an
r- id in the title/description/labels) and Atlas records that cite the id (read-only).

Global options:
      --json   Machine-readable JSON output
  -q, --quiet  Suppress non-essential output
  -h, --help   Show help for this command
