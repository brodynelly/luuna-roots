Usage: luuna-roots sprout <statement> [--file <md>]

Propose a new idea in the agent tree (tier ≥ 2)

The statement is one claim in one line. --file adds prose after it (Markdown).
A sprout never becomes an idea: a human may adopt it (they rewrite it in their own
words) or reject it, in `luuna-roots tend`. Refused below tier 2, at limits.sprouts open
sprouts, or when the statement matches an idea, an open or adopted sprout, or a
rejected one (rejections are permanent). Open sprouts expire after
limits.sproutTtlDays.

Options:
      --file <md>         Prose after the statement
      --slug <slug>       Slug (default: from statement)
      --as agent:<model>  Agent identity (default: $ROOTS_AGENT)

Global options:
      --json   Machine-readable JSON output
  -q, --quiet  Suppress non-essential output
  -h, --help   Show help for this command
