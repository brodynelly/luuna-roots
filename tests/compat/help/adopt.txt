Usage: luuna-roots adopt <sprout-id>

Create a new idea from a sprout (rewrite it, or --endorse it with your thoughts)

Opens $EDITOR on an empty idea.md with the sprout quoted below a scissors
line for reference; everything from that line down is removed on save (also
shown in a split pane under tmux/zellij). Write the idea in your own words;
nothing is copied. Saving nothing above the line cancels.
--endorse keeps the sprout as the agent's text (pinned by hash, quoted and
labeled in view/show/prime/context); you write only your thoughts on it.
Records `<new idea> derives <sprout>` and marks the sprout adopted. Needs an
interactive terminal and a human name (ROOTS_USER or git user.name).

Options:
      --slug <slug>  Slug for the new idea (default: from your first line)
      --no-split     Do not open the sprout in a split pane
      --endorse      Accept the sprout as written; you write only your thoughts on it

Global options:
      --json   Machine-readable JSON output
  -q, --quiet  Suppress non-essential output
  -h, --help   Show help for this command
